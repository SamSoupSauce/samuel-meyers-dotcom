# Crunch

I designed a strategy game on a plate one night with rocks and bottle caps. The idea is to use your player to push rocks. 1 move per turn. Rocks can only move into empty spaces or opponents. The goal is to push a rock into your opponent. Thus; Making them go, **CRUNCH**. I decided it would be more interesting if the board was more than 5x7 and was hexagonal. This is the result. Enjoy! `<3`

# Game Status

Currently, the core rules and 61-space hexagonal board are implemented.
